Delete your Cozy Focus account

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Cozy Focus.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Sync.
  3. Select “Delete account and data”.
  4. Confirm your identity and permanent deletion.

This deletes your Firebase account, synchronized focus history, inventory, room data, badges, profile, and account-scoped local progress.

If you cannot access the app

Email ganyuyku@gmail.com from the same email address used for your Cozy Focus account. Include “Cozy Focus account deletion” in the subject. We may request verification before deleting data.

Retention

Verified deletion requests are processed as soon as reasonably possible. Security logs or backups retained by service providers may remain for limited periods required for security and recovery.
