Privacy Policy

Effective: August 10, 2026

Cozy Focus stores the information needed to provide focus tracking, rewards, room decoration, settings, and optional account synchronization.

Information we process

  • Account identifiers and email address when you use Google or email sign-in.
  • Focus progress, completed session duration, inventory, room layout, badges, profile name, selected built-in avatar, and persistent settings.
  • Anonymous Firebase user identifier when you continue as a guest.

Local and cloud storage

The app works local-first. Account-linked progress is synchronized with Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Firestore data is hosted in the Frankfurt region. Temporary timer state, active audio state, sleep timer state, and test mode remain on your device.

What we do not use

The current version does not use advertising, Firebase Analytics, phone-number authentication, user-uploaded profile photos, or Firebase Storage.

Sharing and retention

We use Firebase as a service provider to authenticate accounts and store synchronized progress. We do not sell personal information. Data remains until you delete your account or request deletion.

Your choices

You can use Cozy Focus as a guest, sign out, or permanently delete your account and synchronized data from Settings. See the account deletion page.
