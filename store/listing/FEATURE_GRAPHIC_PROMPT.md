- `assets/visuals/app_icon_cozy_focus_v3.png`
- `assets/visuals/cozy_focus_room_hero_v2.png`

Prompt:

> Create a polished, wide 1024×500 Google Play feature graphic for Cozy Focus
> that communicates focus plus room design. Show a warmly lit wooden desk and
> chair in a cozy nighttime room, with a crescent moon and garden through the
> window, a desk lamp, books, a small plant and subtle decor. Preserve the dark
> navy, warm wood, sage green and amber identity of the references. Use a wide
> cinematic composition, calm negative space and clean silhouettes. No people,
> loading/progress ring, phone mockup, UI, text, logo or watermark; avoid clutter
> and childish styling.
